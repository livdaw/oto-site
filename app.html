Thinking blocks, held sites and your schedule are managed in the browser extension on each device. This page is your thoughts and today's reflection, synced to your account.

Encrypt my thoughts off

Lock your thoughts with a passphrase only you know. They're encrypted on your device before they're saved, so nobody else can read them: not oto, not the database.

This device remembers your key until you sign out.

Encryption needs a one-time database update before it can be turned on (supabase/notes-encryption.sql in the oto repo).

Connect Claude to your thoughts

Run oto's connector (MCP) so Claude can read and add your thoughts — your originals become the premise it builds on. Needs Node 18+ and the oto-mcp server folder.


      
Claude Desktop → Settings → Developer → Edit Config, paste this, set your password, and restart. In Claude Code: claude mcp add. Point args at your own oto-mcp/index.js.
